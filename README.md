Breaking Bad

Breaking Bad is an American crime drama television series created by Vince Gilligan.

Overview
- Genre: Crime, Drama, Thriller
- Seasons: 5
- Episodes: 62
- Original Run: 2008 – 2013

Main Characters
- Walter White – A chemistry teacher turned meth producer
- Jesse Pinkman – Walter’s former student and business partner
- Skyler White – Walter’s wife
- Hank Schrader – DEA agent and Walter’s brother-in-law

Plot Summary
> “Say my name.”  
> — Walter White

Walter White, a high school chemistry teacher diagnosed with cancer, turns to manufacturing methamphetamine to secure his family’s future. As he dives deeper into the criminal underworld, he transforms into the infamous drug kingpin known as Heisenberg.

## 🏆 Awards
- 16 Primetime Emmy Awards
- 2 Golden Globes
- Widely regarded as one of the greatest TV shows of all time
